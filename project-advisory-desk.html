AI / Multi-agent prototype

Advisory Desk

A consulting workspace designed around specialist AI agents. Users can organize engagements into projects, consult banking, strategy and marketing specialists, and request second opinions.

The problem

Consulting engagements often require input from several disciplines. Advisory Desk explores how a single workspace could organize that input around a shared project.

The concept and features

The concept brings banking, strategy and marketing specialists into one interface. Users can organize engagements, consult individual specialists and request a second opinion.

The approach

AI-assisted prototyping explores how specialist-agent interactions could be presented in a consulting workflow.

Prototype status

Concept and prototype. Not presented as a production consulting service.
