Design / Product prototype

STAGE

An interior-design concept for exploring room layouts, styles and furniture choices, with Room, Photo and Advisor experiences.

The problem

Visualizing how a room might look before making design choices can be difficult. STAGE explores a more approachable way to consider interior design options.

The concept and features

The Room, Photo and Advisor experiences bring together layout, style and furniture exploration within a single product concept.

The approach

AI-assisted prototyping explores how these experiences could be organized into a coherent design interface.

Prototype status

Early-stage product prototype.
